Start a movie night

Name your room. You’ll get a link to invite your friends.

How it works

  1. Invite your group

    Create a room and share the link with up to seven friends. All you need is a nickname.

  2. Choose your tastes

    Each person picks at least three tastes, including one like. Try films, genres, actors, or directors.

  3. Vote for tonight’s film

    When everyone is ready, the host makes a shortlist. Everyone gets one vote.
